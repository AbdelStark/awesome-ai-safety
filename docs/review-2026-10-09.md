# Catalog review: 2026-10-09

This refresh reviewed every entry in the previous catalog for relevance, wording, canonical destination and artifact type. Repository metadata and publisher documentation were checked; selected license files and reproduction instructions were inspected. The review adds missing practitioner workflows and removes overlapping or unsupported recommendations.

The review used AI-assisted research and drafting, followed by editorial primary-source checks. It did not execute the listed benchmarks, validate their results independently, audit their security or establish broad adoption. A working link and a published experiment are different kinds of evidence.

## Gaps addressed

| Gap | Additions | What they let a reader inspect |
|---|---|---|
| Adversarial ML beyond language-model jailbreaks | Adversarial Robustness Toolbox | Evasion, poisoning, extraction and inference attacks, defenses and evaluation interfaces. |
| Runnable evaluations and agent threats | Inspect Evals, PyRIT, AgentDojo, AgentHarm | Task implementations, attack/scorer interfaces, indirect injection and harmful multi-step requests. AgentHarm's purpose restriction is explicit. |
| Behavioral auditing and control | Inspect Petri, Petri Bloom, ControlArena, Monitorability Evals | Simulated audits, seeded behavior tests, control protocols and public monitoring-evaluation splits. These do not establish natural prevalence of misalignment. |
| Cyber and scientific capabilities | CyberGym, Cybench, LABBench2 | Real-software PoC reproduction, CTF environments and scientific research tasks. Keep knowledge, simulated actions and operational outcomes distinct. |
| Interpretation and interventions | circuit-tracer, Gemma Scope 2, OpenUnlearning | Attribution graphs, SAE/transcoder artifacts and comparisons of approximate unlearning methods. |
| Supply-chain and formal properties | Model Transparency, Fickling, Marabou, RFC 9334 | Signed model artifacts, serialized-file inspection, bounded neural-network queries and attestation roles. |
| Governance and assurance | NIST GenAI Profile, SP 800-218A, GPAI Code, OWASP LLM/Agentic guidance, agent security cheat sheet, AISI safety-case template | Official guidance, application threat models and explicit assurance arguments. |
| Research counterexamples and oversight | AI Control, empirical debate, Emergent Misalignment, Unlearning or Obfuscating?, SAEBench, AuditBench, reward-seeker report | Methods and limits beyond refusal scores and preference fit. |

## Corrections and consolidation

- **Training versus safeguards:** moved the Alignment Handbook to post-training. Removed unsupported standard/primary-library language and scale claims from TRL, OpenRLHF and TransformerLens. Removed output quality-guarantee wording from Guardrails AI.
- **Evaluation semantics:** StrongREJECT assesses refusal and harmful-answer usefulness, not just refusal frequency. Anthropic Evals is historical model-written data, not a current dangerous-capability suite. WMDP is a knowledge proxy. Vectara's leaderboard is source-conditioned summarization consistency with a particular judge.
- **Canonical sources:** updated Petri's destination and added Meridian's maintained Petri Bloom. Linked VNN-COMP's competition site rather than only its 2024 repository. Replaced the third-party AI Act overview with the Commission page. Used the published OWASP 2026 editions.
- **Research framing:** corrected Concrete Problems' five topics, including side effects. Scoped power-seeking claims to the paper's assumptions. Bounded Sleeper Agents, Alignment Faking and reward-tampering descriptions to their setups. Moved Claude's Constitution to behavioral specifications.
- **Duplicates:** replaced the broad PurpleLlama entry with its distinct Llama Guard and CyberSecEval destinations. Consolidated auxiliary activation/SAE libraries into current analysis interfaces. Removed the duplicate Anthropic research feed.
- **Navigation:** removed unverified location columns, linked direct curricula, and replaced the undocumented eight-week BlueDot duration with a curriculum description.
